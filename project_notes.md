## Papers

- [Reflecting on naked singularities: iron line fitting as a probe of the cosmic censorship conjecture](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2024MNRAS.528.2015M/abstract) (2024) by Mummery and Ingram. We can try to reproduce some of the figures in this paper.
- [Time machine and geodesic motion in Kerr metric](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00760150) (1978) by Calvani, M., de Felice, F., Muchotrzeb, B. et al. Gives a good overview of causal relations and properties of Kerr-Naked Singularities. Primarily serves as an introduction and a jumping point to further reading.
- Gravitational Collapse and Spacetime Singularities (2007) by Joshi. Has a whole detailed chapter on censorship. Very mathematically in-depth but there are several passages and diagrams providing a descriptive overview.
